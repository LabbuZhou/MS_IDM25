03 / Method

How this archive was assembled

The original study searched the Web of Science Core Collection for works citing the seminal 2000 IDM paper, then applied a target-journal screen and excluded papers that mentioned the IDM only in passing. The resulting focused corpus contained 313 papers.

Network visualizations were produced with VOSviewer. Publication, citation, country, author, and journal values are reproduced from the archived manuscript and therefore retain its database coverage, thresholds, and December 2024 cut-off.

Responsible use. Citation counts describe database records, not intrinsic scientific quality. Rankings should not be used to evaluate individual researchers, countries, or journals without further normalization and context.
